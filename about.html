Our Mission

The Institute for Urban Policy Research & Analysis (IUPRA) serves as a hub for research, teaching, and digital innovation. IRP supports research across public policy, demography, migration, and history through faculty and staff-led research databases, programming, and initiatives. Additionally, by integrating undergraduate research, digital skills training, online curricular innovation, and public-facing scholarship, IRP is developing a scalable model that advances key institutional priorities related to student success, workforce readiness, and research impact. IRP's activities extend beyond traditional research support by creating a curricular ecosystem in which students acquire advanced methodological skills through direct participation in faculty-led research and digital scholarship initiatives.

The Data

The archive has two parts. IUPRA students have compiled historical archival documents from our different areas of research. We also hold census data for Central Texas, which we have cleaned and normalized using our own data methodology.
